moda privacy

moda is a private TV launcher for one household. Its Family Frames screensaver shows photos you choose from Google Photos.

  • It asks Google only for read access to the photos you pick in the Google Photos picker.
  • Photos are streamed to the TV and shown; they are never stored on the TV or sent anywhere else.
  • The Google sign-in is kept encrypted on the TV. Nothing is collected, shared or sold.
  • This website only passes Google's sign-in reply from your phone to your TV on your home Wi-Fi. It stores nothing.
  • You can remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
